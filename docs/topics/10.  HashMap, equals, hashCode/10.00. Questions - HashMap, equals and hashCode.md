This topic focuses on `HashMap`, equality and hashing in Java. It intentionally overlaps with the Collections topic, but goes deeper into buckets, collisions, tree bins, resizing, load factor, mutable keys, `equals()` / `hashCode()` contracts, and concurrency.

The goal is to understand not only how to use `HashMap`, but also why broken equality, poor hash distribution, wrong key design or unsafe concurrent access can cause subtle production bugs.
